# AppointPro – Administrator Manual

## 1. Dashboard & Live Metrics
The AppointPro Dashboard provides real-time KPI visibility computed directly from your database records:
- **Total Appointments**: Lifetime volume of bookings.
- **Today's Appointments**: Count of appointments scheduled for the current calendar day.
- **Upcoming Bookings**: Confirmed upcoming visits.
- **Pending Review**: Bookings requiring manual administrator approval before confirmation.
- **Financial Metrics**: Differentiates between booked revenue, collected payments, and outstanding balances.
- **Upcoming List & Activity Stream**: Recent customer bookings and audit log events.

## 2. Managing Services & Categories
- **Categories**: Organize your service catalogue with custom badge colors and ordering.
- **Service Configuration**:
  - **Buffer Times**: Set buffer before (preparation) and buffer after (cleanup/sanitization).
  - **Notice Rules**: Prevent last-minute bookings using `Minimum Notice Hours` (e.g. 2 hours).
  - **Horizon Limit**: Restrict advance bookings using `Max Advance Days` (e.g. 90 days).
  - **Deposits**: Choose between None, Fixed Amount ($20), or Percentage (20%).
  - **Group Booking**: Set `Capacity Min` and `Capacity Max` to allow multiple seats per session.
  - **Extras**: Define selectable add-on options with additional price and duration.

## 3. Managing Staff & Team Schedules
- **Service Assignments**: Staff can only be booked for services they are specifically designated to provide.
- **Weekly Schedule**: Assign customizable working hours per day of the week.
- **Breaks**: Configure one or more recurring daily breaks (e.g. lunch, coffee break).
- **Holidays & Annual Leave**: Block off individual staff holidays or business-wide closures.
- **Automatic Assignment**: When customers select "Any specialist", AppointPro automatically selects the least-booked eligible staff member for balanced workload distribution.

## 4. Calendar Management
- **Views**: Seamlessly switch between Month, Week, and Day perspectives.
- **Filtering**: Filter by Staff Member, Service, or Location.
- **Rescheduling**: Click any booking to view details, update status, or reschedule to another time with instant collision prevention.
- **iCal / Google Calendar**: Export calendars to standard `.ics` format.

## 5. Customer CRM & History
- View all client profiles, contact numbers, email addresses, and total amount spent.
- Search by customer name, email, or telephone.
- Audit full booking histories and internal administrator notes.
