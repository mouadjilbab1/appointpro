# AppointPro – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Can AppointPro handle group bookings for classes or webinars?
**A:** Yes! Simply open your service settings and adjust `Capacity Max` (e.g. 15 participants). Multiple clients can book seats for the same time slot until maximum capacity is reached.

### Q: Does AppointPro require WooCommerce?
**A:** No. AppointPro is a complete, standalone booking and payment system with native Stripe, PayPal, and offline cash/bank support. WooCommerce is completely optional.

### Q: Does deactivating or updating the plugin erase my customer bookings?
**A:** Absolutely not. AppointPro uses safe versioned migrations. Deactivating and updating preserves all appointments, customer data, and settings. Data deletion only occurs during uninstallation if you have explicitly checked the data removal setting in **AppointPro** -> **Settings**.

### Q: Is AppointPro translation ready?
**A:** Yes. AppointPro includes a standard `appointpro.pot` file, supports standard WordPress localization functions, and includes full RTL (Right-to-Left) stylesheets for Arabic and Hebrew languages.

### Q: Can customers cancel or reschedule their own appointments?
**A:** Yes. By adding the `[appointpro_customer_portal]` shortcode to your website, logged-in clients can view their upcoming appointments, reschedule, or cancel within the allowed cancellation window you configure.
