# AppointPro – Customer Booking & Portal Guide

## Frontend Booking Experience
Customers follow a clear 8-step wizard to book appointments:
1. **Service Selection**: Browse categorized services with clear prices and durations.
2. **Location Selection**: Select preferred branch or clinic (if multi-location is enabled).
3. **Specialist Selection**: Select a specific professional or choose "Any Available Specialist".
4. **Date & Time Picker**: View an interactive calendar showing real-time available slots.
5. **Add-on Extras & People**: Choose extra services and specify the number of participants.
6. **Customer Information**: Provide contact details and custom field responses.
7. **Payment & Coupons**: Apply promo codes and pay via Credit Card, PayPal, or Pay Locally.
8. **Confirmation**: Receive instant on-screen confirmation with a unique booking reference number (`AP-XXXXXX`).

## Customer Self-Service Portal
Embed the portal on any page using the shortcode:
`[appointpro_customer_portal]`

### Customer Portal Capabilities:
- View all past and upcoming appointments.
- Reschedule eligible appointments (respecting minimum notice and cancellation policies).
- Cancel appointments.
- View payment status and receipts.
