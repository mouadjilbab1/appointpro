# AppointPro – Quick Start Guide

Follow these 5 simple steps to get your appointment booking system live in under 10 minutes:

### Step 1: Set Business Information
1. In your WordPress admin, go to **AppointPro** -> **Settings**.
2. Set your **Business Name**, **Contact Email**, **Timezone**, and **Currency**.
3. Choose your preferred slot interval (e.g. 15, 30, or 60 minutes).
4. Click **Save Settings**.

### Step 2: Create Service Categories & Services
1. Go to **AppointPro** -> **Services**.
2. Add your first service (e.g., "60-Min Consultation" or "Dental Checkup").
3. Set the duration, standard price, and any buffer time needed between bookings (e.g., 10 minutes after).
4. Save the service.

### Step 3: Add Staff & Working Schedules
1. Go to **AppointPro** -> **Staff**.
2. Add your team members.
3. For each staff member, select the services they are authorized to perform.
4. Click **Schedule** to define their weekly working hours, off days, and lunch breaks.

### Step 4: Configure Payment Options
1. Go to **AppointPro** -> **Settings** -> **Payment Settings**.
2. Enable **Pay Locally / Offline** for cash or bank transfers.
3. Optionally configure **Stripe** or **PayPal** with your API keys.

### Step 5: Publish Your Booking Wizard
1. Create a new WordPress Page (e.g. "Book an Appointment").
2. Insert the shortcode `[appointpro_booking]` or add the **AppointPro Booking Wizard** Gutenberg block.
3. Publish the page. Customers can now book appointments 24/7!
