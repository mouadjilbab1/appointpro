AppointPro Documentation

Ultimate WordPress Appointment Booking & Scheduling System (Version 1.0.0)

1. Installation Guide

  1. Open the Plugin/ folder inside your purchased package.
  2. Locate the appointpro.zip file.
  3. In your WordPress admin, go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin.
  4. Choose appointpro.zip and click Install Now.
  5. Click Activate Plugin.

2. Quick Start Guide

  1. Go to AppointPro → Settings to set your business name, timezone, and currency.
  2. Go to AppointPro → Services and create your first service.
  3. Go to AppointPro → Staff to add your team members and configure weekly schedules.
  4. Create a new WordPress page and paste the shortcode: [appointpro_booking].

3. Available Shortcodes

  • [appointpro_booking]: Embeds the complete 8-step customer booking wizard on any page or post.
  • [appointpro_customer_portal]: Embeds the self-service customer appointment portal for logged-in users.
  • [appointpro_staff_portal]: Embeds the staff management dashboard for team members.

4. Payment Configuration

  • Offline Payments: Enable in Settings to allow Cash on Arrival and Bank Transfers.
  • Stripe: Enter your Publishable Key and Secret Key in Payment Settings to accept Credit Cards and Apple Pay.
  • PayPal: Enter your PayPal Client ID and Secret Key to accept PayPal payments.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does AppointPro prevent double bookings?
A: Yes! AppointPro features a temporary 10-minute slot reservation lock combined with atomic SQL validation before committing bookings.

Q: Does it work with Elementor and Gutenberg?
A: Yes! AppointPro includes both a dedicated Gutenberg Block and full shortcode compatibility for Elementor, Divi, and other page builders.
